One week (1920)

"One Week" is a 1920 silent comedy short starring Buster Keaton as a newlywed who, with his bride, assembles a prefabricated house sabotaged by jealous rivals, resulting in a whirlwind of absurd structural mishaps and escalating destruction. The film culminates in a storm-ravaged finale where the house, now adrift on a rotating platform, nearly dooms the couple before a heroic save.
